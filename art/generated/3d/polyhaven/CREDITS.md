# Poly Haven assets (CC0)

Downloaded 2026-10-19 from https://polyhaven.com -- public domain (CC0), no
attribution required; credited here anyway.

- Textures (1k jpg, diffuse + OpenGL normal): aerial_grass_rock, grass_ground,
  dry_ground_01, rocky_trail, rock_face
- HDRI sky (2k): kloppenheim_06_puresky
- Card textures for trees and grass (1k): fir_tree_01 twig (diffuse + alpha)
  and bark, tree_small_02 leaves (diffuse + alpha), grass_medium_02 (diffuse +
  alpha). The scanned trees themselves are millions of polygons; only their
  textures are used, on game-style card geometry (game3d/flora.js).
- gravelly_sand (riverbanks).
- waternormals.jpg is from three.js's examples (MIT licence), for ripples.
